# Zerro Trademark Policy

This policy explains how the Zerro name and branding may be used. It is
separate from, and does not change, the copyright license for the source
code in this repository.

## Ownership

The following are trademarks and brand assets of SmartScale Solutions LLC:

- The **Zerro** name.
- Zerro logos and icons.
- Official product branding and official product screenshots.
- The **getzerro.app** domain and website identity.
- Official bundle identifiers, including `com.cbreeding.Zerro`.
- The `zerro://` URL scheme.
- Official code-signing, notarization, release, and software-update
  identities (including the official Sparkle update feed).

trademark policy limits, conditions, or modifies the rights that license
grants you** to use, study, modify, and redistribute the code. In
particular, this policy places no requirements on how you privately use,
modify, or build the source. Copyright licensing and trademark permission
are separate things: the GPL covers the code; this policy covers the Zerro
brand identity.

## Referential (nominative) use

You may use the Zerro name truthfully to refer to this project. For
example, you may:

- Say that your project is "a fork of Zerro" or "based on Zerro".
- Say that your build was "compiled from the Zerro source code".
- Write articles, reviews, tutorials, or documentation about Zerro.
- Link to this repository or to getzerro.app.

Referential use must not suggest that your project or build is the official
Zerro app or is endorsed, sponsored, or maintained by SmartScale Solutions
LLC.

## Private builds

Building Zerro from source for your own use requires no rebranding. You do
not need to rename anything, change identifiers, or alter branding in a
build you compile and use yourself and do not distribute.

## Distributed builds (forks and modified versions)

If you distribute a build of this code to other people (modified or not),
you must give it its own identity:

- Choose a different product name (not "Zerro" or a confusingly similar
  name).
- Replace the Zerro logos and icons with your own.
- Use your own bundle identifiers (not `com.cbreeding.Zerro` or other
  `com.cbreeding.*` identifiers used by official builds).
- Use your own URL scheme (not `zerro://`).
- Sign and notarize with your own identity and, if you ship updates, host
  your own update feed. Do not point builds at the official update feed.
- Do not present official Zerro branding or screenshots as depicting your
  build.
- Avoid anything that suggests endorsement by SmartScale Solutions LLC.

These requirements exist so that users can tell official Zerro releases
apart from community builds, including for security updates and support.
