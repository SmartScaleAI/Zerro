# Provider logo sources

The onboarding provider marks are the vendors' supplied marks, reduced only to
the vector artwork needed by the macOS asset catalog:

- `ProviderOpenAI`: OpenAI's monochrome API/service mark from the official
  OpenAI brand surface.
- `ProviderGemini`: Google's Gemini sparkle path and Aurora palette from the
  official Google-hosted Gemini asset.
- `ProviderAnthropic`: the Ivory Anthropic symbol from Anthropic's official
  media resources archive.

Retrieved July 30, 2026. The marks remain the property of their respective
owners and must not be recolored or otherwise modified without rechecking the
current vendor guidelines.
